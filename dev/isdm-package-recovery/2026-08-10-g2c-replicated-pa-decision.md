# G2c decision: a fresh three-visit PA recovery gate

## Decision

Approve G2c as a fresh, private package-native recovery design with three
conditionally independent PA visits at the same linked cells.  It is a
predeclared information-design change, not a retry of the one-visit campaign:
the previous ordinary result remains `G2_PACKAGE_PA_HOLD`, and its invalid
disconnected attack remains invalid.

The frozen protocol is
`2026-08-10-g2c-replicated-pa-protocol.md`.  It fixes the generating law,
three-visit support, ordinary seeds `81101:81120`, attacks paired to ordinary
primitives `r = 1:5`, eligibility (including the gradient bound), fixed
`log_psi` profiles, recovery thresholds, immutable root rules, and claim fence
before a runner or launcher exists.

## Why this is the next admissible design

The retained one-visit ordinary panel established only that its exact design
did not meet the joint recovery gate.  Three repeat PA observations can add
information while preserving the ecological process, source supports, offsets,
free-`Psi` estimand, package route, and first PA visit.  The paired `one_visit`
and `three_visit` construction makes that change auditable: it isolates added
conditionally independent PA observations rather than silently changing truth
or support.

## Guardrails and rejected alternatives

- Do not alter the previous result root, its denominator, or its verdict.
- Do not call three visits a detection model or claim detection calibration:
  there is no detection parameter in the G2c law.
- Do not weaken recovery thresholds, drop profile failures, or select an
  eligible-only denominator after seeing results.
- Do not let an attack pass silently.  Both attack maps and their degradation
  are required for an ordinary PASS.
- Do not add count, spatial, comparator, empirical, source-admission, public
  API/docs, or article work to this lane.
- Do not launch Totoro until the new runner's paired local smoke has produced
  and passed its required immutable receipts.

## Authorisation boundary

This decision authorises S0--S4, including the local smoke, and the bounded
private implementation needed to realise the frozen protocol.  It does **not**
authorise Totoro S5.  That campaign requires a fresh explicit go-ahead after
the runner, launcher, no-write validation, and local-smoke receipts have been
reviewed.
