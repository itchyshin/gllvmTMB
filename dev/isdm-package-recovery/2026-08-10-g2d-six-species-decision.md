G2d is approved as a fresh private implementation of the frozen protocol in
`2026-08-10-g2d-six-species-protocol.md`.  It changes only the community
dimension: it retains G2c species 1--3, adds species 4--6 at the stated truth,
and retains the GBIF Poisson plus three linked PA-cloglog route, rank-one
ecological covariance, free diagonal `Psi`, relative-intensity estimand, and
recovery targets.

G2c remains `G2C_SMOKE_ADMISSION_HOLD`.  Its roots, seeds, results, and
verdict must never be reused or reclassified.  G2d starts from the independent
ordinary seed block `86101:86120`; paired attack primitives are only `1:5`.

The implementation must retain all attempts and profile evaluations, reject a
non-finite objective before eligibility, validate the weak-overlap map from
the fixture itself, and make all six `theta_diag_B` profile verdicts named.
The implementation, no-fit validation, and one fresh local smoke are
authorised.  Launching Totoro, changing package code or public surfaces, and
widening to count, spatial, comparator, source-admission, empirical, or Issue
#953 work are not authorised by this decision.  A healthy smoke requires a
separate Totoro approval.
