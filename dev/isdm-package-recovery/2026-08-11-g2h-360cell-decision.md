# G2h preparation decision record

G2h is admissible for a future redesigned local smoke only when the no-fit
fixture proves all frozen geometry conditions: 360 cells, six species, three
visits, GBIF-only `B`, \(|cor(x,b)|\le0.10\), rank three for `(1,x,b)`, and all
six conditional GBIF-bias information values at least 130. Failure of any
condition is `G2H_PREFLIGHT_HOLD` and permits no substitute fixture.

A future smoke must be separately approved and must retain the exact fit,
three restarts, six profiles, decision ledger, failure placeholders, and
manifest. It cannot authorize a campaign.
