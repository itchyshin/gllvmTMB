# Integrated-JSDM requalification lane

This directory is the isolated preparation surface for public-route
integrated-JSDM evidence. It does not certify a capability by itself. Production
fits may start only after the coefficient series releases its shared paths and
one green `origin/main` commit, tree, installed package, loaded DLL, and relevant
source hashes have been frozen together.

The lane calls `gllvmTMB(..., family = isdm_sources(...))` only. Historical
private `.gll_isdm_fit()` and direct `TMB::MakeADFun()` campaigns remain negative
or private provenance and cannot satisfy these gates.

## Immutable programmes

`contract.R` generates the plans rather than reading mutable CSV input:

- 1,600 ordinary nonspatial attempts and 200 disconnected-support attacks,
  seeds 202608280--202610079;
- 800 held-out spatial attempts, seeds 202610080--202610879;
- 4,800 base uncertainty attempts, seeds 202610880--202615679;
- 14 point and 24 uncertainty pre-run fits outside production denominators.

No attempt is replaced. A started record is written before fitting. Every
returned fit, error, interruption, and unavailable environment/source outcome
has a terminal record. The summarizer reports planned, started, terminal,
eligible, and target-specific denominators separately.

The 1,800 nonspatial campaign denominator is split before adjudication: 1,600
ordinary attempts can promote the tested regime and 200 disconnected-support
attempts are stress evidence only. Full/weak pairs share a structural DGP seed
but retain distinct registered attempt seeds and observation draws.

## Current boundary

Preparation code and tests are safe to develop under the disjoint lease. The
source qualification, public wording repairs, claim-bearing fits, interval API,
shared validation register, package documentation, and closeout remain blocked
by the coefficient lease or by their preceding scientific gates.
