## 2a. Mathematical Contract

The ordinary latent decomposition remains

\[
\Sigma = \Lambda\Lambda^\mathsf{T} + \Psi,
\]

with diagonal \(\Psi\); `latent(..., unique = FALSE)` remains the
loadings-only subset. The default integration engine remains native Laplace.

One numerical repair was admitted: eligible native-Laplace `nlminb` fits with
code zero, positive-definite Hessian, no boundary flag, and raw maximum gradient
at least 0.01 receive one warm restart. Acceptance requires a strictly smaller
raw gradient, preserved health, and objective no worse than 64 machine epsilons.
This does not relax the convergence gate or repair boundary/weak-identification
cells.

For a loading ridge with scale \(\tau\), the selected parameter is a MAP point
minimising

\[
F_{\mathrm{opt}}(\theta) = F_{\mathrm{lik}}(\theta) +
\frac{1}{2\tau^2}\lVert\theta_{\Lambda}\rVert_2^2.
\]

The fit now stores `likelihood_nll`, `ridge_penalty`, and `optimization_nll`
separately. `logLik()` returns \(-F_{\mathrm{lik}}\) at the MAP point and warns
that this is not the unpenalised maximum. No likelihood family,
parameterisation, covariance grammar, estimator default, or interval claim was
promoted.

## 9. What Did Not Go Smoothly

- The original seven heavy failures looked platform-specific but were shared
  fixture/test defects: two SPDE meshes used a brittle cutoff and the bootstrap
  comparator had too few surviving draws.
- Campaign v2 and v3 gate designs failed adversarial review several times:
  detector sensitivity had no positive challenge denominator, manifests were
  not always consumed, component schemas were inferred from observed rows, and
  broad closeout omitted whole campaigns. Production waited until these failed
  closed.
- The exact-aa campaign produced a broad HOLD, not the hoped-for universal core
  pass. Gaussian latent small-n, NB2 small-n, missing-response robustness and
  detector sensitivity were genuine limits.
- Two full-suite attempts were intentionally superseded by later review/source
  changes. The final run was kept frozen and completed cleanly.
- The last issue audit found six further source/claim defects after an apparent
  pass, including the route-specific `opt$objective` semantics. Fixing those
  materially improved the release boundary.

## 12. Cross-Product Coverage

This arc **does NOT cover** general interval calibration, power, all-family
recovery, ordinal certification, structured-tier certification, mixed-family
certification, VA/AGHQ/EVA promotion, Julia parity, or exact-candidate platform
evidence.

- **Ordinary scientific core:** Gaussian `indep`, `dep`, and selected `latent`;
  Poisson, NB2 and binomial-logit ordinary latent cells were attempted at every
  frozen size/regime. Only cell-specific point-estimation passes are claimed.
- **Robustness:** missing-response and rare-level cells were executed for all
  four core families; failed cells remain visible and narrow the claim.
- **Silent failure:** Gaussian and binomial regimes crossed sample size, loading
  scale and missing-response level. Detector sensitivity failed broadly and is
  not promoted.
- **Comparators:** matching Gaussian modes and Poisson/NB2/binomial latent routes
  were compared with `glmmTMB`/`gllvm`; Gaussian fixed-coordinate densities were
  compared with independent Stan implementations.
- **Inference boundaries:** weighted objectives, ridged MAP fits, VA, AGHQ, EVA,
  ordinal and structured tiers each have an explicit public status and a
  fail-loud or documentation fence. No general coverage claim was added.
- **Penalty/engine propagation:** ridge status is covered by `print`, `summary`,
  `logLik`, AIC/BIC warnings and stored objective components for native Laplace
  and AGHQ. It does NOT cover a validated effective-df criterion, penalised LRT,
  VA ELBO information criteria, or ridge-calibrated intervals.
- **Weighted-objective propagation:** point-estimate labels and all exported
  SE/interval entry points are covered. It does NOT cover sandwich covariance,
  cross-validation-based weight choice, likelihood-ratio inference, or
  calibrated weighted prediction uncertainty.
- **Missingness/aggregation:** exact-aa includes missing-response and rare-level
  robustness cells with attempt-level identities. It does NOT cover modelled
  missing predictors combined with VA/AGHQ, unconditional structured-tier
  bootstrap redraw, or broad missingness coverage.
- **Platforms:** local macOS source evidence is complete for this arc; three-OS
  CI and exact candidate checks remain downstream evidence and are not inferred
  from the local pass.
