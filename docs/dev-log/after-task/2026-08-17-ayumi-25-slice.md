## Mathematical contract

Mapped-off components: when a trait's unit-tier (`diag_B_skip`) or OLRE-tier
(`diag_W_skip`) variance is deliberately suppressed, its reported `sd` is the pinned
plumbing constant `exp(log 1e-6)`, not an estimate; boundary diagnostics must screen
only the estimable subset. The two masks are the complete inventory (verified by grep
over R and C++; both lengths C++-guaranteed to `n_traits`).

Objective provenance: `report$joint_nll_*` are CONDITIONAL JOINT quantities on the TMB
tape (they differ only by TMB-side MSPL penalties, inside `estimator_id == 1`);
`fit$objective_components` is the MARGINAL surface and the only place the R-level
loading ridge appears. On an ML ridged fit `joint_nll_penalized ==
joint_nll_unpenalized` is a structural identity — pinned as a test, documented in
roxygen.

**What this is NOT:** no change to any estimate or optimisation path; no change to the
C++ report fields; no `se.fit` machinery added to `fitted()` beyond forwarding `...`
to `predict()`; not the Ayumi #23 screening work; not a reply to Ayumi.
