## 1. Goal

Deliver a single exact-byte gllvmTMB 0.7.1 release candidate after three
narrow closures: warn when optional grouping slots are supplied but unused
(#1190), make the legacy Sigma extractors visibly soft-deprecated (#1194), and
state a conservative VA boundary (#1189). The endpoint is retained candidate
evidence, not a tag, CRAN submission, or public release.

## 3a. Decisions and Rejected Alternatives

- **Decision:** fence only new 0.7.1 prose while retaining older MSPL and
  random-slope documentation. **Rationale:** maintainer authorization and the
  claim review found the retention/provenance distinction clear. **Rejected
  alternative:** delete or rewrite historical material, which would erase
  evidence and widen this release. **Confidence:** high.
- **Decision:** retain the two R CMD check NOTEs and one unused-helper compiler
  warning as explicit limitations. **Rationale:** all platforms passed and the
  narrow release does not justify unrelated API/Rd/compiler cleanup.
  **Rejected alternative:** byte-changing repair and a new candidate cycle.
  **Confidence:** high.
- **Decision:** close #1190 and #1194; leave #1189 open. **Rationale:** the
  first two approved closures are complete, whereas #1189's proposed
  low-prevalence VA recommendation needs deferred empirical work. **Rejected
  alternative:** present the VA fence as a calibrated recommendation.
  **Confidence:** high.
