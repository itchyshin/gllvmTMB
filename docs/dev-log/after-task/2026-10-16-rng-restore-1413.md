# After task: seeded functions restore the caller's RNG (#1413)

## Scope
- `simulate.gllvmTMB_multi()`, `bootstrap_Sigma()` and
  `gllvmTMB_check_consistency()` called a bare `set.seed(seed)`, leaving the
  caller's global stream in the seed-derived state. They now call the new
  internal `.gllvmTMB_local_seed()` (`R/rng-utils.R`), which saves
  `.Random.seed`, seeds, and restores on exit (also on error). Seeded results
  are unchanged; `seed = NULL` is a no-op.
- Not changed: the other bare `set.seed()` sites listed in the issue
  (`bootstrap-lv-effects.R`, `coverage-study.R`, `cv-internal.R`,
  `julia-bridge.R`, `loading-ci-bootstrap.R`). They were source-only in the
  report and are left for a follow-up.

## Checks
- No R in the authoring sandbox: nothing ran locally. No roxygen block
  changed, so `devtools::document()` should produce no output. Verification is
  CI: red proof (test on base) then green on the fix.
- `tests/testthat/test-rng-restore-1413.R` fails on base for all three
  functions and for the helper.
