## 1. Goal

Fit two named Gaussian kernel tiers with separately requested trait-specific uniqueness, or stop explicitly when the requested model is unsupported. Preserve structural missing cells and include fixed observation error once.

## 2. Implemented

The working-tree implementation admits a narrow fixed-error Gaussian path, records both named uniqueness tiers and refuses unsupported combinations. It fixes `known_sigma_eps` as a positive scalar standard deviation. It refuses unconditional simulation and new-data simulation for multiple named kernels, and conditional new-data prediction for those kernels, pending support for their random effects. Fixed-only new-data prediction and conditional training-row simulation passed one fitted-object check.

### Mathematical contract

For tier `s`, `Sigma_s = Lambda_s Lambda_s^T + diag(psi_s)`, with `psi_s = exp(2 log_sd_s)`. The observed covariance is the sum of `Sigma_s` paired with source-aligned and symmetrized `K_s + 1e-8 I`, plus `known_sigma_eps^2 I` once, restricted to observed cells. This changes admission and fixed-error mapping in the R wrapper; it does not claim calibrated inference, mixed-family support or Julia parity. The rank-two same-trait kernel screen is not a proof of identification.

## 3a. Decisions and Rejected Alternatives

**Decision:** Preserve both requested uniqueness tiers or reject the fit. **Rationale:** public main silently omitted both, and the older development fallback could omit one. **Alternative:** warn and fit a reduced model; rejected because it changes the requested covariance. **Confidence:** high.

**Decision:** Reject unsupported unconditional multi-kernel simulation and conditional new-data prediction. **Rationale:** the existing paths do not redraw or re-add the named kernel effects. **Alternative:** silently proceed; rejected. **Confidence:** high for the observed paths; the exact-source focused suite confirmed both refusal messages.
