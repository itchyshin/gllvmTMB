Three modes: indep = per-trait diagonal · dep = full unstructured ΣT (T(T+1)/2 params) · latent = ΛΛT with optional diagonal Ψ companion. Ordinary latent() carries Ψ by default; source/kernel latent terms are loadings-only unless unique = TRUE. The scalar (one shared trait variance) case is the modifier common = TRUE on any indep term — e.g. indep(0 + trait | g, common = TRUE), intercept-only. The scalar()/*_scalar() keywords are soft-deprecated compatibility syntax for it.

Where the gaps are · 0.5.0 → 1.0

  • Interval coverage — the headline gap. Zero families have coverage-checked intervals; every cell is point-only. Calibration is the largest 1.0 task.
  • Random slopes (≥1) are unfinished. In development / diagnostic-grade even for Gaussian — not a finished 0.5.0 capability. Shown as partial, not ✓.
  • Missing responses: core families now tested. Gaussian + poisson/NB2/binomial masked-response is verified (sentinel-invariance, 2026-07-12); nbinom1 recovery + mi() predictor coverage pending.
  • Fitted diagnostics reach 3 families (Gaussian, Poisson, NB2). Extending residual / predictive checks to more families is the next diagnostics step.
  • Categorical needs major development — nominal support is early-stage, not a usable path yet.
  • REML is a Gaussian-only pilot. Non-Gaussian, weighted, and missing-data REML are later work.
  • Delta / hurdle latent-scale correlation awaits a reporting convention (two response scales) — a design gap, not a bug.
  • Grammar symmetry: the covariance grid is three modes {indep, dep, latent}; the one-shared-variance ("scalar") case folded into the common = TRUE modifier on any indep term (the scalar()/*_scalar() keywords are soft-deprecated compatibility syntax). The correlated-vs-uncorrelated (| / ||) random-slope axis is now built: mode(1 + x || g) = intercept ⟂ slope (indep|| fully diagonal, dep|| = Σint⊕Σslope via a Cholesky parity pin, latent|| the separate-Λ form) across phylo / animal / kernel / spatial + source-tier latent, recovery/target-matrix tested (Design 79 §4).

Reconciled against NAMESPACE family exports, NEWS.md (diagnostics scoped to Gaussian/Poisson/NB2; single-trial Bernoulli diagonal RE mapped off; Student-t variance NA for df ≤ 2), the keyword grid, and the README support boundary, plus maintainer-confirmed status for the in-progress lanes (random slopes ≥1 unfinished; missing responses validated for Gaussian only; categorical early-stage). Where per-family structured or slope support is not individually validated in code, the column reflects grammar availability, not a coverage claim — firming these into per-cell evidence is a 1.0 audit, not a 0.5.0 promise.

The ladder follows docs/design/80-nongaussian-re-evidence-bars.md. A large ML-vs-REML gap is the cheap diagnostic that flags the small-cluster regime where the variance estimate is untrustworthy — the natural hook for the AGHQ work.

On VA / EVA. Both exist as sealed research engines with no export, no method= argument and an untouched NAMESPACE; Laplace remains the only estimation route. They are listed here so the ladder is complete, not because they are available. Measured 2026-07-26/27 against gllvm 2.0.13 on 640 grid cells and a 48-cell scale sweep: agreement is exact where the algebra matches (Poisson 4.4e-09, binomial Jaakkola–Jordan 2.7e-07 median relative difference), but VA is not faster than Laplace at any tested size — the variational arms scale roughly quadratically in n against Laplace's linear. The one property that favours them is honest failure: across those 640 cells VA never reported a clean status on a degenerate fit, whereas 59 of 70 degenerate Laplace fits reported convergence = 0 with pdHess = TRUE. Their prospective role is therefore a cross-engine second opinion, not a faster or more accurate estimator. Nothing here is promoted, and none of it gates 0.6.

Update 2026-07-29 — a THIRD engine, and the slowness is diagnosed. The 2026-07-26/27 slowness is not a property of VA: it is the optimisation architecture. va_r3 hands all N(2q + q(q−1)/2) variational coordinates to one quasi-Newton solve (26,985 at n = 5397, q = 2). A three-arm A/B on that engine (dev/vgh/ab-runs/) shows moving the block to random= is invalid (it estimates a biased ΣB, rel. Frobenius 0.65–0.74, flat in n) and profile= is correct but 4–7× slower — though with linear per-evaluation cost and flat memory against the status quo's n1.35 and quadratic memory. Two corrections to the paragraph above: the status-quo arm did complete at N = 3200 (895.6 s — the earlier non-completion is not reproduced), and its binding limit is memory, not time (1340 Mb at N = 3200; projected 9.3 GB at N = 10,000).

A block coordinate-ascent engine (R/va-vgh.R, internal, unexported, added 2026-07-29) solves each block instead — closed-form variational covariance from Stein/Price, a free Newton step in the mean, and a response-separable model step, so no optimiser sees more than p + q coordinates. It computes the identical objective to va_r3 (4.7e-15 over 46 comparisons, 7 negative controls firing) and reaches the same estimates (binomial n = 800, 12 seeds, paired difference bounded within ±0.0003 on a metric of level 0.218 — an equivalence result), at ~17× per start. SQUAREM acceleration then cut poisson from 450 sweeps to 77 at the same ELBO, closing the one real defect. Gaussian ELBO equals the exact marginal log-likelihood to 1.35e-15. Still internal, unexported, and not an estimator: the intended role is to warm-start Laplace and to screen the degenerate fits Laplace reports as clean, with the published estimate remaining Laplace's. Nothing promoted; none of it gates 0.6. See docs/dev-log/2026-07-29-vgh-report.md.

Read against the grid at the top of this page, that is 3 families of 30+, one mode of three, one source of five, and no missing-data support — VA/EVA cover a small corner of what Laplace already fits. Most of the gap is not conceptual: one 1-D Gauss–Hermite rule admits any family whose latent variable enters through a scalar linear predictor (docs/design/104–105), and multi-tier, random slopes and structured priors all accumulate additively into the same µ and v (docs/design/106). Missing data is the one gap that blocks real datasets outright today.
