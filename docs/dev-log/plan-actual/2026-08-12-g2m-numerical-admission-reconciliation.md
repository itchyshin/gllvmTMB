## Plan versus actual

G2m was a prospective, design-only numerical-admission decision. It created no
fit, objective, profile, simulation, campaign, or external-compute state. The
validator reads text only and exits before any engine-loading or model call.

The final protocol supplies the requested decision table, conditional polish
rule, `NO_CANDIDATE` Case C outcome, symbolic alignment, adversarial
validation/provenance requirements, local-pre-run gate, and independent review.

## Reconciled decision

`NOT_REQUIRED` is valid prospectively for raw-gradient-passing,
polish-ineligible fits. The existing G2i boundary candidate remains conditional.
Case C is `NO_CANDIDATE`, not because no algebraic primitive exists, but because
the covariance-Newton primitive is boundary-only and lacks safeguards needed to
identify/admit it for non-boundary `b_fix`/`theta_rr_B` geometry.

G2k was not mutated or reclassified. Its likelihood, DGP, seed grid, source
gate, parameter map, raw threshold, recovery metrics, and
`G2K_CALIBRATION_HOLD` remain unchanged. G2c remains
`G2C_SMOKE_ADMISSION_HOLD`.

## Authority boundary

This record authorizes only the design. A local pre-run requires separate
approval after implementation, pure-logic and compiled-unit validation, and
independent method/claim review. No campaign follows automatically.
