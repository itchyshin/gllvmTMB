**Date:** 2026-08-08  
**Owner:** Rose claim-fence audit  
**Release lane:** `cursor/cran-0.7-20260807`  
**Decision state:** binding wording guide for the 0.7 release edit; it does not

## How to use this matrix

This document converts the validation-debt register into public-claim rules for
`DESCRIPTION`, `NEWS.md`, `README.md`, roxygen/Rd, articles, startup text, and
`cran-comments.md`. Internal artefacts cite register IDs. Reader-facing prose
uses the required plain-language boundary and does not expose register IDs.

The labels below have fixed meanings:

- **release core**: may appear in the first-use story at the stated scope;
- **scoped**: implemented, but the named boundary must travel with the claim;
- **experimental**: opt-in research route; availability is not certification;
- **historical**: evidence from a superseded route; never evidence for the
  current default;
- **blocked**: must not be implied by an export, parser keyword, internal engine,
  or future plan.

“Covered” in the register may mean routing, algebra, a smoke fit, or a
point-recovery cell. It does **not** mean calibrated uncertainty unless the
register names repeated-sampling evidence for that exact estimand and regime.

## Release-wide approved wording

Use this as the top-level uncertainty boundary:

> Point estimates are the package's primary inferential output, but their
> evidence is route- and regime-specific. Broad interval calibration remains
> incomplete. Three exact native pinned unrotated ordinary-Gaussian standardized-loading
> symmetric joint-delta Wald cells have target-specific certificates:
> `(n=150,d=2)`, `(n=400,d=1)`, and `(n=400,d=2)`, restricted to structurally
> free strict-lower targets. These are one frozen DGP, conditional on eligible
> fits; no other truth-parameter regime inherits the result. Total-variance
> penalty profiles remain route-only.
> Every other returned interval must be described by target, method, and status.

Use this as the integration boundary:

> Native TMB Laplace is the default. AGHQ and VA are opt-in experimental routes.
> AGHQ is ridge-penalised at `tau = 2` by default once AGHQ is enabled; use
> `aghq_ridge = Inf` for unpenalised AGHQ. The current VA route uses GH with
> `H = 7`, returns an ELBO rather than a marginal likelihood, and labels its
> available uncertainty `calibrated = FALSE`. EVA is not an admitted integration
> value.

Use this as the missing-data boundary:

> Missing response cells can be masked on the native route, and VA has a narrower
> experimental response-mask route. One explicitly modelled `mi()` predictor is
> supported only on the native Laplace route and only for the named predictor
> models. Multiple `mi()` terms, VA `mi()`, MI pooling, MNAR models, and the
> broader deferred missing-data extensions are not supported.

Use this as the #750 boundary:

> Unconditional simulation redraws only the documented random-effect tiers.
> SPDE and diagonal phylogenetic tiers currently fall back with a warning to
> conditional simulation; simulate-refit intervals from that fallback are too
> narrow and must not be treated as calibrated.

## Words that require a named object

The following terms are forbidden without the named object shown here:

| Term | Required object |
|---|---|
| `default` | The actual function formal and dispatch path |
| `covered` | A current validation-register row at the same estimand and regime |
| `calibrated` | A repeated-sampling campaign with estimand, regime, denominator, failures, Monte Carlo uncertainty, and register disposition |
| `certified` | Only the exact three CI-13 standardized-loading cells and structurally free strict-lower targets named in the terminal interval ledger, for its frozen DGP and conditional on eligible fits |
| `95% coverage` | No retained interval campaign earned an empirical 0.95 floor; the exact CI-13 certificates concern nominal-95% intervals passing the frozen 0.94 and lower-band gates |
| `unconditional bootstrap` | A simulator route that redraws every active random-effect tier |
| `ML` | An unpenalised likelihood optimum; an AGHQ fit at finite ridge is MAP |
| `current VA evidence` | Evidence generated under the current public GH evaluator, not the historical JJ route |

Do not use “calibrated 9-node,” “no cell's interval coverage is certified,”
“the ridge is never applied unless named,” “VA supports `mi()`,” or
“`bootstrap_Sigma()` fails loudly for `spde`/`phylo_diag`” in 0.7 prose.

## Unresolved adjudications

These are decision points, not permission to widen prose:

1. **Fisher — VA 36,000-fit campaign.** `NEWS.md` now reports the 20/36
   fixed-effect and 15/36 latent-SD results as campaign pass/fail outcomes, not
   calibration certificates. VA-06, VA-09, and VA-13 remain partial and public
   objects remain `calibrated = FALSE`. Fisher must still decide whether the
   campaign earns any register-row movement and, if so, the exact
   family/rank/estimand/status wording. Safe default: no promotion.
2. **Resolved 2026-08-25 — PI-02 is not an exception.** The exported helper is
   retained as `route-only`; its historical and PVT-02 numerical passes do not
   establish exact constrained-refit fidelity. Only the three exact CI-13 cells
   named above carry target-specific certificates, for the frozen DGP and
   conditional on eligible fits.
3. **Fisher — AGHQ estimator language.** The safe release claim is integrator
   correctness plus partial failure/runaway evidence, not estimator or coverage
   certification. Any stronger phrase requires a register update tied to a fair
   ridge-matched comparator.
4. **Grace — release identity and generated artefacts.** The 0.7 bump must update
   `DESCRIPTION`, the current NEWS heading, README/package citation,
   `inst/CITATION`, and `cran-comments.md` without rewriting historical
   deprecation versions. Roxygen changes must regenerate matching Rd before the
   tarball check.
